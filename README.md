# 📅 Biblical Calendar

**A Christian calendar designed to help you follow the Biblical calendar alongside the Gregorian calendar.**

Biblical Calendar is more than a calendar — it is a daily companion for Biblical time, Scripture study, prayer, worship, family worship, encouragement, and spiritual reflection.

It brings Biblical days, months, Sabbaths, feasts, new moons, daily astronomical times, and Bible-centered daily features together in one Android application.

## ✨ Features

- 📅 Biblical Calendar alongside the Gregorian calendar
- 🕊️ Weekly Sabbaths
- ✡️ Biblical Feasts and appointed times
- 🌙 New Moons
- ☀️ Twilight Start
- 🌅 Sunrise
- 🌇 Sunset
- 🌌 Twilight End
- 📖 Bible content and daily Biblical information
- 🌍 Multiple languages
- 📱 Android application
- ⚙️ Personal calendar and localization settings
- 🔐 Secure personal data and backup architecture

## 📖 Spiritual & Bible Features

- 📜 **Daily Biblical Promise** — every day, the app provides a randomly selected Biblical promise from a collection of **7,000+ promises gathered from the Bible**.
- ⚠️ **Daily Admonition** — a daily Biblical admonition for reflection, correction, and spiritual growth.
- 📚 **Random Bible Chapter Study** — the app randomly selects real Bible chapters for study during **prayer, worship, and daily Family Altars**.
- 💬 **Up to 12 Daily Encouragements** — up to **12 encouragements** can appear randomly on the screen throughout the day.
- 🙏 **Prayer, Worship & Family Altar Study** — Bible chapters are randomly selected for these daily moments, providing a different study experience over time.

## 🔐 Secure Personal Vault

- 🛡️ **Strong security architecture** for protecting the user's private data.
- 🔑 **The key on paper (26 characters) belongs only to the user** and is never stored in the application's cloud backup.
- ☁️ Backups stored on Google Drive contain only **encrypted** data, never the user's key.
- 📱 After bringing a profile onto another device, the user opens it with the key on paper and then uses the device's hardware-backed security for everyday access.
- 🚫 The user remains in control of the key required to recover the vault.

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Personal & Family Features

- 📔 **Daily Journal** — a private daily journal protected by **PIN, fingerprint, or password**.
- 🎂 **Birthdays of Loved Ones** — add the birthdays of family members and loved ones to the calendar, including their **photo**.
- 📅 **Automatic Yearly Birthday Display** — once a birthday is entered, the algorithm displays it every year on the exact corresponding date in the calendar system you choose:
  - 📜 **Biblical Calendar System**
  - 🏛️ **Gregorian (Roman) System**

## 🎯 Purpose

The goal of Biblical Calendar is to provide a practical way for Christians to explore and follow the Biblical calendar while keeping the familiar Gregorian calendar available for reference.

The application is being developed with a focus on accuracy, privacy, security, and practical everyday use.

## 📱 Android

Biblical Calendar is being developed for Android.

A public release and Google Play availability will be announced when ready.
